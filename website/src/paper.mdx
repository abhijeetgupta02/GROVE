<Section id="how" title="How it works">

A vision-language decision model (<span class="font-mono">d3:27b</span>) answers <Num k="setup.question_count_h" as="int" /> fixed yes/no questions about the photo with a probability each: a main question per category, fact questions (for example, an unprotected edge or a worker without a hard hat) and protection questions. A formula with weights frozen before any test image was scored combines them into one score per category, and a threshold per category turns the score into an alert. For each alert a fixed phrase is sent to GroundingDINO (with DETR and OpenCLIP as a fallback) to find the evidence box. Click through the steps for one selected example.

### Evidence boxes

<Section id="limitations" title="Limitations">

- **Balanced image sets.** Half of the calibration and final images contain a violation, far more than in practice. Standardized to the natural violation rate (<Num k="final.reference_rate" d={3} />), precision is <Num k="final.grove.std_precision" /> for GROVE against <Num k="final.gemma.std_precision" /> for <span class="font-mono">gemma4:31b</span>: at that rate most alerts of either system would be false.
- **Narrower rules in ConstructionSite10k.** Its labels follow its own rules: high-visibility vests are required only at night, and falls count only from heights or depths of <Num k="dataset.height_rule_m" as="text" /> m or more. GROVE&rsquo;s questions are broader, so some of its alerts are &ldquo;wrong&rdquo; only under these rules.
- **No Electrical labels** outside the development images, so Electrical is not tested on new images.
- **Calibration needs labeled images from the target site.** Thresholds carried over from the development images gave micro-F1 <Num k="final.first_freeze_grove.micro_f1" /> on the final images<PostHoc />; the refit thresholds needed <Num k="cal.n_images" as="int" /> labeled calibration images.
- **Speed.** The decision step takes about <Num k="speed.decision_seconds" d={1} /> s per image on one workstation, against <Num k="speed.single_pass_seconds" d={1} /> s for the single-pass model.

</Section>
